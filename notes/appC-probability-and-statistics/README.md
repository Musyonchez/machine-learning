# Appendix C: Probability and Statistics

Transcribed notes from Appendix C ("Probability and Statistics") of *Data Science and Machine Learning* by Kroese, Botev, Taimre, and Vaisman. This appendix reviews the probability and statistics background used throughout the book: probability spaces, random variables and distributions, expectation, conditioning and independence, transformations, the multivariate normal distribution, limit theorems, Markov chains, and the core ideas of statistical inference (estimation, confidence intervals, hypothesis testing).
